# 🚀 AlphaLens

### Turning Market Signals Into Opportunities

AlphaLens is an AI-powered market intelligence platform that helps users analyze market trends, identify opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

Powered by Meta's open-source AI ecosystem and TinyLlama, AlphaLens transforms complex business and market data into actionable insights through intelligent analysis and prediction.

## Features

- 📈 Market Trend Analysis
- 🎯 Opportunity Detection
- 🏢 Competitor Insights
- 🤖 AI-Powered Recommendations
- 📊 Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Technology Stack

- TinyLlama
- Ollama
- Flask
- HTML, CSS & JavaScript
- Python

## About

AlphaLens was developed for the **Meta Global AI Hackathon**.

Our mission is to help entrepreneurs, startups, investors, and businesses discover opportunities before everyone else.
